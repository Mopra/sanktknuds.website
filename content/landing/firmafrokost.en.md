Sankt Knuds is a place to gather, also when work is what brings you together. We host groups of 8 to 60 guests for lunch, dinner or an evening in the bar, and we tailor the event to your occasion.

## Why companies choose Sankt Knuds

- **Right in the city centre.** Ryesgade 29 is a few minutes' walk from Aarhus Central Station, so guests from across the country can get here without a car.
- **Lunch from 11:30.** Our [lunch menu](/en/lunch) is served Monday to Saturday from 11:30 to 16:00, and the evening kitchen opens at 17:30.
- **From snacks to several courses.** Start with oysters, caviar or Argentine prawns, then continue with dishes from the [evening menu](/en/menu).
- **The bar afterwards.** We're open until 23:00 Monday to Thursday and until 02:00 on Fridays and Saturdays, with [cocktails](/en/cocktails) and a broad [wine list](/en/wine).

## Occasions we help with

Company lunches, team lunches, client dinners, receptions, anniversaries and farewell lunches. Planning a Christmas party? We have a set [Christmas lunch menu at DKK 495 per person](/en/christmas-lunch).

## How to book

Write to us with the date, time, number of guests and any wishes for the menu or allergies, and we'll get back to you with a proposal. You're also welcome to call if it's urgent.
