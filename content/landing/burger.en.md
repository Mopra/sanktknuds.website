It's one of the dishes guests mention most often when they write about us afterwards. The patty is ground from beef and lamb, and the onion gets to play the lead.

## What you get

- Patty of beef and lamb
- Fresh onions, caramelised onions and fried onions
- Tangy onion cream and applewood cheddar
- Cornichons and shaved cabbage
- French fries

**Price: DKK 225**

## When to have it

The burger is on both the [lunch menu](/en/lunch), served Monday to Saturday from 11:30 to 16:00, and the [evening menu](/en/menu) from 17:30. It goes well with a cold draught beer or a glass of red from the [wine list](/en/wine).

We're at Ryesgade 29 in central Aarhus, a few minutes' walk from Aarhus Central Station. Booking is recommended, especially on Fridays and Saturdays.
