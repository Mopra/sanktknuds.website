Going to Musikhuset or Aarhus Theatre? Sankt Knuds is a natural place to start the evening. We're at Ryesgade 29 in central Aarhus, so you can eat without rushing and walk the rest of the way.

## Distance

- **Musikhuset Aarhus:** under 10 minutes' walk
- **Aarhus Theatre:** about 10 minutes' walk
- **Aarhus Central Station:** a few minutes' walk if you arrive by train

## Making it in time

The evening kitchen opens at 17:30, Monday to Saturday. If the show starts at 19:30, a table at 17:30 or 18:00 is the most relaxed. Mention in your booking when you need to leave and we'll plan the evening around it.

For something lighter, share a few snacks and starters from the [evening menu](/en/menu), such as oysters, Argentine prawns or [hand-stirred beef tartare](/en/menu/tatar).

## After the show

The bar is open until 23:00 Monday to Thursday and until 02:00 on Fridays and Saturdays. Come back for a [cocktail](/en/cocktails) or a glass from the [wine list](/en/wine) once the curtain falls.

Book early on nights with big shows.
