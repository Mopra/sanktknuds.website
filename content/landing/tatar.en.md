Tartare is a brasserie classic. We stir ours from beef with dehydrated tomatoes, crisp Jerusalem artichoke and a mushroom mayo. It's rich, fresh and crunchy at the same time.

## How we serve it

- **As a starter:** DKK 155
- **As a main:** DKK 255

At lunch it's on the [lunch menu](/en/lunch) as hand-stirred beef tartare for DKK 155. In the evening you'll find it on the [evening menu](/en/menu) as both a starter and a main.

## In the glass

Tartare goes well with a light red or something sparkling. Ask your server, or browse the full [wine list](/en/wine).

We're open Monday to Saturday at Ryesgade 29 in central Aarhus. Lunch is served from 11:30 to 16:00, and the evening kitchen opens at 17:30.
