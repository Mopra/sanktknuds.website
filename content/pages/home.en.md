Going to Musikhuset or Aarhus Theatre? Sankt Knuds is a natural place to start the evening. We're at Ryesgade 29 in central Aarhus, so you can eat without rushing and walk the rest of the way.

The evening kitchen opens at 17:30, Monday to Saturday. If the show starts at 19:30, a table at 17:30 or 18:00 is the most relaxed.

The bar is open until 22:00 Monday to Thursday and until 01:00 on Fridays and Saturdays. Come back for a cocktail or a glass from the wine list once the curtain falls.
