Sankt Knuds Brasseri & Bar sits at Ryesgade 29 in the heart of central Aarhus — a few minutes' walk from the Latin Quarter, Aarhus Cathedral and the pedestrian high street.

## By train and public transport

Arriving by train, it's a short walk up Ryesgade from Aarhus Central Station — the street begins at the station and leads straight to our door. That makes us an easy place to meet if you're coming from opposite ends of the country, or if there's an hour to spend before departure.

The light rail (Letbanen) and city buses stop nearby. Bike racks are right outside the door.

## Parking

There are several public car parks within walking distance of Ryesgade. If you're driving, it's usually easiest to park near the station and walk the last stretch.

## When we're open

We're open from lunch until late every day except Sunday. The kitchen serves [lunch](/en/lunch) from 11.30 to 16.00, and the dinner menu from 17.30. The bar stays open late on Fridays and Saturdays.

Reservations are especially recommended on Fridays and Saturdays.
